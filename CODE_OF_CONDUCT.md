# Code of Conduct

This project is a free, open-source cost calculator. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented — is expected to follow this code.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background, experience or identity.
- Assume good intent, and give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and back pricing claims with a source.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, and personal attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, [@gaikwadashutosh35](https://github.com/gaikwadashutosh35), through a GitHub issue — or, if the matter is private, by asking in an issue for a private contact. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, in proportion to what happened.
