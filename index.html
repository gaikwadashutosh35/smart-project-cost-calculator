How does the hosting cost calculator work?

  1. Describe the project. Pick a type (AI SaaS, e-commerce, mobile app, marketplace, API…) and its features, or describe it in your own words.
  2. Estimate users. Choose expected users, how often they use it and where they are. The calculator turns this into monthly active users, requests, database operations, storage and bandwidth — all editable.
  3. Add AI only if you need it. Choose usage level and models; it prices input, cached and output tokens per model, and MCP server hosting for agents.
  4. Set a budget and priority. Lowest cost, balanced, performance or enterprise reliability, plus a preferred cloud and currency.
  5. Compare three architectures. Budget, Recommended and Scalable, each with every service, why it is needed, the formula behind its cost and the official pricing link.

Frequently asked questions

Is this hosting cost calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is free to use and needs no account. Your scenarios are saved in your own browser.

Are the prices live?

They are a dated snapshot. Each price was read from the provider's official pricing page on 1 November 2026, and every estimate links to that page. Prices that could not be confirmed are labelled as estimates.

Does the estimate include payment gateway fees and taxes?

No. Payment gateways such as Razorpay and Stripe charge a percentage of your revenue, so they are listed separately, and taxes such as GST or VAT are excluded.

Why doesn't the cost grow in a straight line with users?

Free tiers run out at different points, plans step up, servers are added only when peak traffic needs them, and AI token costs grow with active users rather than sign-ups. The growth simulator re-runs the full estimate at each user count.

Which currencies does it show?

Indian rupees, US dollars, euros and British pounds, converted at European Central Bank reference rates of 30 October 2026.
