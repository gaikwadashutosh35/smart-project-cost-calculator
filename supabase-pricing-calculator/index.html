Supabase pricing calculator

Supabase Pro costs $25 a month per project. That includes Micro compute, an 8 GB database, 250 GB of egress, 100,000 monthly active users and 100 GB of file storage. Above those quotas you pay per unit — $0.125 per GB of database, $0.09 per GB of egress and $0.00325 per extra monthly active user. The Free plan costs nothing but caps the database at 500 MB and pauses projects after a week of inactivity.

What do the Supabase compute add-ons cost?

Paid plans include $10 a month of compute credit, which covers one Micro instance. A larger size is billed at its list price minus that credit, so the Pro total grows with the size you pick. Each additional project needs its own compute, starting at $10 a month.

When is the Supabase Free plan not enough?

  • You run it in production. Free projects are paused after one week of inactivity; Pro projects are never paused.
  • Your database passes 500 MB or your app sends more than 5 GB of data a month.
  • You pass 50,000 monthly active users or 1 GB of stored files.
  • You need more than 200 realtime connections at once or 2 million realtime messages a month.
  • You need more than two active projects — the Free plan allows two.

What makes a Supabase bill grow?

On Pro, the $25 fee covers most small apps; the bill grows with four things. Monthly active users above 100,000 cost $0.00325 each, so 100,000 more users add $325. Egress — every byte your database API and file downloads send out — costs $0.09 per GB above 250 GB, so 1 TB a month adds $67.50. Database disk above 8 GB costs $0.125 per GB, and a bigger compute size adds $5 to $100 a month.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Supabase cost per month?

Supabase Pro costs $25 a month per project, including Micro compute, an 8 GB database, 250 GB of egress, 100,000 monthly active users and 100 GB of file storage. Usage above those quotas is billed per unit, and larger compute sizes cost extra.

Is Supabase free?

Supabase has a Free plan with a 500 MB database, 5 GB of egress, 50,000 monthly active users and 1 GB of file storage. Free projects are paused after one week of inactivity, so it suits testing rather than production.

How much does Supabase cost for 100,000 users?

Pro includes 100,000 monthly active users, so authentication adds nothing up to that point. Each monthly active user above 100,000 costs $0.00325, so 200,000 monthly active users add $325 a month. Database size, egress and compute usually grow at the same time.

Do I pay separately for Supabase Auth?

No. Authentication is part of the plan, and monthly active users are billed only above the plan's quota (50,000 on Free, 100,000 on Pro).

What does the Supabase compute credit cover?

Paid plans include $10 a month of compute credit, which covers one Micro instance. Larger sizes are billed at their list price minus the credit: Small adds $5, Medium $50 and Large $100 to the $25 Pro fee.
